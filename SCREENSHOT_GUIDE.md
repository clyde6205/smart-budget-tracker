# 📸 Screenshot & Demo Video Shot List

Practical, ready-to-use guide for producing the visuals your listing
needs. This can't be generated without the live app running — use this
as your checklist once you've deployed and run through the smoke test.

## Why This Matters More Than It Seems

A Gumroad-style listing with no real product screenshots converts
dramatically worse than one with 4–6 clean shots or a short walkthrough.
This is often the single highest-leverage thing left to do once the
product itself works — worth doing before writing more copy.

## Screenshots to Capture (5, in this order)

1. **Dashboard, fully populated** — log in with realistic-looking
   sample data (a few weeks of varied transactions, not just the
   defaults), so the charts and breakdown actually look alive. This is
   your hero image — the one that appears first in the listing.
2. **The Personal/Business toggle, mid-switch** — capture the moment
   right after clicking it, so it's visually obvious this is ONE app
   doing two jobs. This is your #1 differentiator vs. generic budget
   templates — make sure it's visually unmistakable in the listing.
3. **AI Insights or the "Ask a question" box, with a real answer
   showing** — this is the proof-of-AI shot. Don't screenshot the empty
   input; screenshot it with a real question and a real, specific
   answer visible.
4. **Debt Calculator or Goals tab, showing a progress bar or payoff
   timeline** — visual progress bars/timelines convert better than
   tables of numbers; pick whichever looks more "finished" once you've
   entered a couple of realistic entries.
5. **Setup & Account, scrolled to Sharing & Collaborators** — if you're
   marketing this to small business owners, this screenshot alone can
   justify a higher price point, since almost no competitor at this
   price offers multi-user access.

## 60-Second Walkthrough Script (Loom-style)

Don't write a script you'll read word-for-word — bullet points to hit,
said naturally:

**0:00–0:10 — Hook**
"If you've ever wanted one budget app that handles both your personal
money AND a side business, without paying for two subscriptions —
watch this."

**0:10–0:25 — Show the switch**
Open the Dashboard, click the Personal/Business toggle live on camera.
"Same login, two completely separate books. Nothing crosses over."

**0:25–0:40 — Show the AI**
Type a real question into "Ask a question about your finances," let the
answer appear on screen. "It's not generic advice — it's reading your
actual numbers."

**0:40–0:50 — Show one more differentiator**
Pick whichever is most relevant to your audience: the debt payoff
calculator, the recurring transactions auto-posting, or the
sharing/collaborator feature.

**0:50–0:60 — Close**
"Everything you just saw is in the file — link below." (No need to
narrate every tab; showing restraint here reads as confidence, not as
hiding something.)

## Practical Notes

- Record at 1080p minimum; Gumroad and most platforms compress video
  hard, so starting soft-looking makes it worse.
- Use realistic dummy data, not "Test Test Test" or "asdf" — a viewer's
  brain registers realistic numbers as trustworthy almost instantly.
- If you're not comfortable on camera, a screen-recording with captions
  and no voice works fine — the visual proof matters more than narration
  polish.
