# 🚀 Quick Start — Read This First

Welcome! This takes about 2 minutes and covers everything you need to
get real value from your first session.

## 1. Log In and Find Your Bearings

You already created your account with your license key. Log in, and
you'll land on the **Dashboard** — the numbers you see are **sample
data**, not yours. That's intentional, so you can see how everything
looks filled in before you touch anything.

## 2. Replace the Sample Data With Your Own

Go to the **💳 Transactions Log** tab. You can either:
- Add entries one at a time with the form at the top, or
- Click into the table below it and edit/delete the sample rows directly

Don't worry about getting categories perfect on the first pass — you
can always adjust them later, and if you've added your own AI key (see
step 4), the app will suggest categories for you as you type.

## 3. Personal or Business? Both.

See the toggle right under the header — **🏠 Personal** and **💼
Business**? These are two completely separate books under one login.
Use Personal for your own money, Business for a side hustle or company,
and switch between them anytime. Nothing from one leaks into the other.

## 4. Optional: Turn On AI Features (2 minutes, free to try)

Go to **⚙️ Setup & Account** → **AI Features**. If you have (or get) a
free Anthropic API key from console.anthropic.com, paste it in there.
This unlocks:
- **AI category suggestions** when logging a transaction
- **AI-generated insights** on your Dashboard
- **Ask a question about your finances** in plain English

Skip this entirely if you'd rather not — the app is fully functional
without it.

## 5. Set Your Real Numbers

Still in **Setup & Account**, set your actual currency, starting
balance, payday cycle, and budget year — these currently show defaults.

## 6. Explore the Rest at Your Own Pace

- **📅 Monthly Budget** — see spending by category across the year, set
  annual targets.
- **🔁 Recurring** — set up rent, payroll, or a subscription once; it
  posts itself automatically from then on.
- **🎯 Goals** — track progress toward a savings target with a visual
  progress bar.
- **🧊 Debt Calculator** — see a realistic payoff timeline for any debts.
- **📈 Net Worth** — log assets and liabilities, save snapshots over
  time to see your trend.
- **🏦 Bank Accounts** (top of Transactions tab) — if you're in a
  supported country (US, Canada, UK, parts of Europe), you can connect
  a real bank account instead of entering everything by hand.

## Common First Questions

**"Can someone else help me manage this?"**
Yes — in Setup & Account, under "Sharing & Collaborators," you can
invite someone (a bookkeeper, a partner) to a specific book as a Viewer
(look only) or Editor (can add/edit, can't change settings).

**"Is my data safe?"**
Your password is encrypted, never stored in plain text. You can turn on
two-factor authentication in Setup & Account for extra security.

**"What if I want a printable/accountant-ready report?"**
Setup & Account → Data Management → Generate PDF Report.

**"What if I want out?"**
You can delete your account and all its data permanently anytime, from
Setup & Account.
