## Long-Form Listing Description (Gumroad / Etsy style)

**🚀 The Budget Tracker That Actually Keeps Up With You — Personal AND Business, One Account, Anywhere You Log In**

Most budget templates are a spreadsheet you download once and hope you
remember to open. This is a real, hosted app: log in from your phone,
laptop, or anyone's browser, and your data is exactly where you left it
— because it's backed by a real database, not a fragile file that resets
if something goes wrong.

**🏠💼 Two Full Books, One Login**
Track your personal finances and a side business — or a full-time one —
without mixing the two or paying for two separate tools. Switch between
Personal and Business tracking anytime with one click. Business mode
even swaps in the right framework: instead of the 50/30/20 rule, you get
a 60/25/15 operating-cost breakdown built for how a business actually
spends — payroll, marketing, taxes, reserves.

**✨ Optional AI Superpowers**
Add your own AI key (in Setup, entirely optional) and unlock AI-powered
category suggestions when you log a transaction, plus one-click spending
insights that read your numbers and tell you what actually matters —
in plain English, not a spreadsheet full of formulas to interpret
yourself.

**🔒 Real Accounts, Real Security**
Bank-style password hashing, a private account only you can access, and
full self-service password recovery by email if you ever forget it — not
a shared file anyone with the link can edit.

**📊 Everything a Serious Tracker Should Have**
- Live income/expense/cashflow dashboard with a visual budget breakdown
- Full 12-month expense aggregation with custom annual targets
- Debt payoff calculator (Avalanche or Snowball strategy) with real
  payoff timelines and total interest — and it won't crash or show you
  nonsense numbers if a payment can't cover the interest
- Net worth tracker with historical snapshots and a trend line, not just
  a single number
- Fully customizable categories for both Personal and Business books

**💰 Get Started**
Your license key unlocks full access — one key, one account, yours to
use for as long as your license is active.

## Positioning Note

Lead with "hosted app with accounts" and "Personal + Business in one,"
since that's the real differentiator against the sea of static budget
spreadsheets already on these marketplaces — that comparison is what
should make a buyer choose this over a $5 Google Sheet. Don't undersell
it by describing it the way you'd describe a spreadsheet template.
